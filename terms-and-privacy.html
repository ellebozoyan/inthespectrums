3. Privacy

The short version

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any information about you. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking pixels, no advertising, and no analytics. Everything you type into a tool on this site stays on your own device. The single exception is the optional translation service described below, which does nothing until you choose a language.

What we collect: nothing

This site has no accounts and no forms that submit anywhere. We do not use cookies for tracking, we do not run advertising, and we do not use third-party analytics. We do not build profiles, and we have no ability to identify you.

The one exception, and it is yours to make

The Language button offers to translate the site using a service run by Google. That service is not loaded unless you choose a language. If you never use it, no outside company is involved in your visit at all.

If you do choose a language, your browser contacts Google to fetch the translation, and Google will know you visited that page. Your choice is remembered on your own device so you are not asked again; Back to English in that panel clears it. Nothing you type into any tool on this site is ever sent, translated or otherwise.

Your browser's own translation, which the panel recommends first, involves no third party added by us at all.

What the tools store, and where

The interactive tools save what you enter using your browser's local storage — a feature of your own browser, on the device that you are currently using. That information is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, cannot retrieve it, and cannot delete it for you.

To delete it yourself, use the delete or reset option inside the tool, or clear your browser's site data for this site.
