Or start with what is actually happening

The thing you would say out loud, rather than the name of a service. Most of these lead to more than one place, because most of them have more than one cause.

Eating and the gut

  • Eating very few foods — feeding and eating, the food list
  • Losing foods that used to be fine — feeding and eating, eosinophilic esophagitis
  • Not eating enough to grow — nutrition formulas and how to get them
  • Gagging, choking, food sticking — eosinophilic esophagitis, feeding
  • Constipation, or holding on — what actually helps, the chains it starts
  • Stomach pain nobody can explain — abdominal migraine, the whole picture

Sleep and energy

  • Cannot fall asleep, or wakes all night — sleep, what is worth asking about
  • Snoring, mouth breathing, restless sleep — the airway
  • Exhausted no matter how much sleep — dysautonomia, the whole picture
  • Tires far faster than other children — physical therapy, low tone

Behavior and distress

  • Hitting, biting, throwing — behavior is communication, in the moment
  • Meltdowns that seem to come from nowhere — the stacking, precursors
  • Refusing everything that is asked — demand avoidance, the choice planner
  • Hurting themselves — behavior is communication, picking and pulling
  • Running off, wandering, no fear — safety
  • Anxious, worried, stuck on things — anxiety and OCD

Communication

  • Not talking, or very few words — speech, language and AAC
  • Talking, but not understood — speech and AAC, apraxia
  • Cannot tell you what is wrong — communication access, boards for mealtimes
  • Understands far more than they can say — why a device
  • Seems not to listen — auditory processing, hearing

Senses and movement

  • Covers ears, hates noise or light — sensory, occupational therapy
  • Flapping, rocking, spinning — sensory and self-regulation
  • Bumping into things, missing what is there — CVI
  • Clumsy, falling, poor balance — physical therapy
  • Handwriting, buttons, cutlery — occupational therapy
  • Toe walking, unusual gait — physical therapy

Learning and attention

  • Cannot read, or reading is a battle — learning and literacy
  • Cannot start, finish, or remember steps — executive function
  • Bright, but school is going badly — the whole picture, inside the IEP
  • Losing skills they used to have — the conditions library, and ask for assessment

Medical and unexplained

  • Pain nobody can find a cause for — chronic pain, the whole picture
  • Headaches, or head pressing — migraine
  • Seizure-like episodes — epilepsy, functional neurological disorder
  • Ill far more often than other children — when something happens, asthma and allergy
  • Symptoms after an illness or injury — acquired brain injury, PANS and PANDAS
  • Being told nothing is wrong — the whole picture, reading a test result

Systems and money

  • School will not give what is needed — inside the IEP
  • A claim was denied — paying for it
  • No idea what we are entitled to — programs and entitlements
  • Turning eighteen soon — after school ends
  • Too many appointments to hold in my head — the family calendar

The rest of the household

  • I am running out — the people doing the caring
  • My other child never asks for anything — siblings
  • A sibling is getting hurt — when it is not safe or fair
  • Nobody understands what our week is like — the share builder
