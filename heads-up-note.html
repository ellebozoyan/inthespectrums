Why a note at all

The thing it actually solves

Most people want to get it right and do not know how to ask. Asking feels like admitting they think something is wrong, so they say nothing, guess, and then feel awkward about the guess.

A short note from you removes that entirely. It is not a warning and it is not an apology. It is the same courtesy as mentioning a nut allergy before a birthday party — useful information, given lightly, so everybody can relax.

What makes one land well

  • Short. If it needs scrolling it will be skimmed. Three or four sentences is plenty.
  • Concrete over diagnostic. "He'll cover his ears if the room gets loud, and that's fine" tells somebody more than the name of a condition, and is nobody else's business besides.
  • Say what to do, not what to avoid. "Just carry on as normal" beats a list of things not to say.
  • One thing they can actually do. People relax enormously when given a job.
  • End warmly and without apology. You are not asking a favor. You are making the afternoon go better for everybody, including them.
  • Do not over-explain. A note that reads as anxious makes the reader anxious.

Whose information is it

Every note here describes somebody who did not write it. Before sending, ask whether they would be comfortable with this person knowing this — and where they are old enough to have a view, ask them.

Share the minimum that makes the situation work. A sitter needs to know about seizures. A friend's parent hosting a playdate for a sibling does not need a diagnosis, a history, or anything beyond what will come up in the next three hours.

See their own voice on who gets to tell somebody's story.

If somebody wants more than a note, for everyone else is a whole page written to be sent — and the share builder makes a proper document for anyone providing care.
