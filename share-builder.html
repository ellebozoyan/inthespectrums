The share builder:
you decide what leaves the house

A new aide doesn't need medical history. A gastroenterologist doesn't need handwriting goals. This assembles a packet from whatever you've logged in the other tools, with you ticking exactly what goes in — then prints it or saves it as a file you hand over deliberately.

Nothing here is ever transmitted. There is no account, no server, and no link to send. Sharing means you print a page or download a file and give it to someone — which is why you have complete control over what it contains.

That is more control than most sharing systems give you: this is section-by-section, not document-by-document. See using these tools for how to save it as a PDF on your device.

Backup and restore

One file covering every tool on this site. Download it every few weeks — this is the only copy that exists.

A word about what you choose to include. There is no obligation to share medical information with a school, a coach, or a babysitter, and no obligation to share behavior records with a doctor who hasn't asked. Share what will help the person receiving it do their job better — and no more than that.

Where a person is old enough to have a view about what gets shared and with whom, ask them. Their own voice covers how that shifts as they grow, and why it eventually stops being your call.

Reading this as a professional

If a family hands you a packet from this, they chose each section deliberately. Absence of a section is not absence of information — it means they decided it wasn't yours to have, which is their right.

If you need something that isn't there, ask for it specifically and say why. That is a better conversation than assuming you have the whole picture.

Take this page, change it, host it yourself. It is one file with nothing behind it, and it works offline.
